About BiteWise

Know before you bite.

Learn why we built BiteWise and how our AI screening engine works to simplify dining out with food allergies.

The Problem

People living with food allergies face constant uncertainty when dining out. Restaurant menus frequently leave out key ingredients, rely on vague dish descriptions, or fail to highlight allergens hidden in marinades, sauces, or glazes. This leaves diners performing manual inspection and repeatedly questioning staff.

The Solution

BiteWise acts as an AI-powered screening assistant that extracts actual dishes from menu photos or text input and analyzes food context semantically. Dishes are grouped into 🟢 Likely Safe, 🟡 Check First, and 🔴 Avoid, giving diners instant clarity while emphasizing responsible safety guidance.
